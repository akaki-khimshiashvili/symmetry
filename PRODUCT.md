# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

- Public site: Astro (static output, bilingual routes, Georgian default at `/`, English at `/en/`).
- Admin panel: Vite (vanilla TS), Georgian-only UI.
- Backend: PHP 8.1+ JSON API with SQLite (PDO), file uploads for product photos.

## Users

- Restaurants and cafés, offices and commercial spaces, hotels — buying a statement wall for a space guests see.
- Interior designers specifying a piece for a client project.
- Individual homeowners wanting a premium centrepiece wall.
- Internal: Symmetry staff managing the catalogue and incoming order requests through the admin panel.

## Product Purpose

Symmetry designs and produces parametric 3D wooden wall panels and wall art in Georgia. The site exists to raise brand awareness and to organise orders: a visitor browses products, picks a size, sees the price for that size, and sends an order request or contacts the studio (WhatsApp / email / phone).

## Positioning

Parametric, computationally designed relief panels cut from real wood and produced locally in Georgia, with delivery and installation handled by the studio.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive mostly on phones from social media; designers and businesses also on desktop.
- Contact preference matters: WhatsApp is a primary channel in Georgia.
- Staff update products (photos, materials, size variants with prices, delivery time, installation time) in Georgian.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual content (ka / en) for every product field and page. Georgian is the default language.
- Product types: portrait (vertical) and landscape (horizontal).
- Pricing: each product has fixed size variants (width × height), each with its own price in GEL.
- Product detail: photos, sizes, price, delivery and installation time, material, order-request button, social/phone contact.
- Contact form — required: first name, last name, phone, client type, preferred channel (WhatsApp / email). Optional: intent (order vs question), message.
- Payment: Flitt integration is planned as the final phase. Until then, orders are submitted as requests (no online payment).
- Admin: add / edit / delete products; manage description, material, sizes + prices, delivery time, installation info; view order requests and contact messages.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Symmetry (Latin wordmark in both languages).
- User-pinned direction: minimal, modern, luxurious and extravagant; palette from luxury wood tones; high-value typefaces; minimal content-loading animations. Multiple wood palettes must be switchable so the owner can choose.

## Product Principles

1. The wood is the hero — interface recedes behind material and relief.
2. Every product page answers "how big, how much, how soon" without scrolling hunts.
3. Contact is always one tap away, on the visitor's preferred channel.
4. Georgian first, English equal — never a lesser translation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast on all palettes; reduced-motion respected; Georgian script must render in chosen faces.
